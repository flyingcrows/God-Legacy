LEGAL INFORMATION

Terms of service

Terms must identify the legal seller, explain ordering and acceptance, prices and currencies, payment, shipping, cancellations, returns, product information, warranties, liability, dispute handling and governing law, subject to mandatory consumer protections.

Complete before launch

  • Legal seller name, registered/contact address and jurisdiction.
  • Countries served and applicable consumer protections.
  • Order acceptance, pricing, taxes, duties and payment rules.
  • Shipping, cancellation, returns and warranty terms.
  • Support and complaint process.
  • Appropriate governing-law and dispute provisions.

This is a preparation page, not final legal terms. Obtain qualified review before accepting orders.
