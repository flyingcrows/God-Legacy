# GOD LEGACY — Supplier verification checklist

A supplier is not approved just because a marketplace profile exists. Record evidence for each item.

## Identity and legitimacy
- [ ] Legal business name, registration and operating address checked
- [ ] Business contact and bank/payee name consistent
- [ ] References, operating history and independent reviews checked
- [ ] Product origin and brand/IP rights clarified

## Product and quality
- [ ] Exact model/specifications/materials/dimensions confirmed
- [ ] Sample ordered and inspected before sale
- [ ] Safety/compliance documents appropriate to product and destination countries received and checked
- [ ] Product photos/video licensed for store use
- [ ] Packaging, manuals, warranty and language confirmed

## Dropshipping and fulfilment
- [ ] Single-unit order fulfilment confirmed in writing
- [ ] No supplier invoices/promotional inserts unless agreed
- [ ] Target countries and shipping services confirmed
- [ ] Processing and realistic delivery estimates confirmed
- [ ] End-to-end tracking and carrier handoff confirmed
- [ ] Duties/taxes and customs declaration responsibilities clarified
- [ ] Lost, damaged, defective, cancellation and return processes documented
- [ ] Live stock updates and price-change notification agreed
- [ ] Backup supplier or alternative fulfilment route identified

## Commercial checks
- [ ] Sample, unit, packaging and shipping costs recorded
- [ ] Payment terms and minimum order quantities confirmed
- [ ] Gross margin calculated after gateway fees, refunds, duties/taxes and support costs
- [ ] No unsupported health, performance or delivery claims
- [ ] Recheck supplier before scaling

**Approval rule:** Do not publish a product as available until supplier, sample, landed cost, shipping, returns and relevant compliance checks are complete.
