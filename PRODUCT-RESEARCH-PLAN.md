# GOD LEGACY — product discovery and validation plan

## Brand categories under consideration

The existing category architecture is a starting hypothesis, not a commitment to list every category:
- Smart gadgets
- Fitness tools
- Everyday essentials
- Wellness/recovery
- Travel/tech organisation

## Better entry strategy

Do not compete head-on with mass-market stores on commodity electronics. Test one micro-niche where product usefulness, content and curation can differentiate the brand.

Initial hypotheses to research:
1. Cable and desk organisation for hybrid workers/students.
2. Compact travel organisation for carry-on travellers.
3. Small-space fitness accessories for home workouts.

These are hypotheses, not proven winners. They require keyword/search-demand, competitor-price, review-complaint and supplier-cost checks before selection.

## Product validation checklist

- Customer problem is clear and recurring.
- Search demand exists beyond one viral social post.
- Competition can be differentiated by bundle, use case, instructions, customer service or target audience.
- Product is lightweight, durable, non-fragile and not regulated/high-risk.
- No trademark/counterfeit or misleading performance claims.
- Two independent supplier quotes are available.
- Exact landed cost and destination shipping are known.
- Margin remains positive after all variable costs and a refund reserve.
- Supplier accepts one-piece fulfilment and provides tracking.
- Product can be described honestly without fake scarcity, fake reviews or invented discounts.

## Unit economics

For every product and destination calculate:

Customer price
− supplier product cost
− supplier shipping/handling
− payment processing and currency conversion
− taxes/duties paid by seller (where applicable)
− expected refunds/chargebacks/defect reserve
− platform costs (target ₹0)
= contribution before marketing and owner labour

If the contribution is not positive, do not list the product. If the supplier needs to be paid before the payment processor releases customer funds and the business has no cash buffer, do not accept the order.

## Research output required before product launch

For each shortlisted SKU maintain:
- supplier legal/business name and source URL
- exact product page / SKU
- supplier quote date and currency
- product cost and shipping cost by destination
- origin warehouse
- dispatch and delivery estimates
- stock confirmation
- tracking details
- return address and defect/loss policy
- competitor price range and evidence
- customer problem / differentiation angle
- contribution calculation
- final pass/fail and reviewer date
