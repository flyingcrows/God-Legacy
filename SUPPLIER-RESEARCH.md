# GOD LEGACY — supplier research (preliminary shortlist)

Research date: 18 November 2026  
Budget rule: $0 / ₹0 supplier subscriptions, paid directories, inventory purchases, samples or advertising.  
Status: Research shortlist only. No supplier is approved until its exact SKU, landed cost, destination, delivery estimate, returns terms and payment timing are confirmed in writing.

## Strategy: differentiated demand, not simply obscure suppliers

A supplier being less popular does not automatically make a product profitable. GOD LEGACY should look for under-served *customer problems* and specific use cases, then source a dependable product. Avoid generic “viral gadget” lists, trademarked/counterfeit goods, safety-critical electronics, supplements, cosmetics with unsupported claims, batteries where shipping restrictions are unclear, and products with high return risk.

Initial positioning to test:
- Compact everyday organisation for commuters, students and remote workers
- Practical travel organisation (non-electrical, low breakage)
- Simple fitness accessories (non-medical, no therapeutic claims)
- Print-on-demand brand merchandise only if designs are original and audience-led

Do not launch all categories at once. Start with one coherent micro-category and 3–5 supplier-verified products.

## No-monthly-fee sourcing candidates

### 1. CJdropshipping — first general-product sourcing candidate
- Public site: https://cjdropshipping.com/
- Public positioning: product sourcing and fulfilment platform; free account entry is advertised.
- Potential use: request sourcing for a specific product that is not widely listed; compare warehouse origin and shipping lanes.
- Must confirm: exact SKU cost, pick/pack/service fees, shipping charge, dispatch time, tracking, destination restrictions, return address and refund rules.
- Caution: “free account” does not mean free products or free fulfilment. Product quality and shipping vary by SKU and route.

### 2. EPROLO — second general-product candidate
- Public site: https://eprolo.com/
- Public FAQ states no membership/monthly subscription fee; seller pays product and delivery when placing an order.
- Potential use: compare sourcing and fulfilment quotes against CJ for the same product.
- Must confirm: exact product origin, actual stock, delivery route, tracking, defect/return process and whether any branding service is optional/paid.
- Caution: do not rely on general “global shipping” wording; verify the specific country and SKU.

### 3. Printify — print-on-demand candidate (not a generic product wholesaler)
- Public site: https://printify.com/pricing/
- Public pricing lists a $0 Free plan. Merchants pay the product's production cost and shipping when orders are fulfilled.
- Potential use: original GOD LEGACY artwork on simple apparel/accessories; no inventory is bought in advance.
- Must confirm: provider's location, production time, shipping to destination, base cost + shipping + tax, and the cash-flow timing.
- Caution: it is not truly cash-free. Print provider charges the merchant to produce/ship; merchant needs funds before customer payouts may arrive.

## How to find genuinely less-saturated products

1. Start from customer complaints and use cases, not a supplier's “best sellers”.
2. Search specific long-tail phrases and review recurring complaints about existing solutions.
3. Prefer simple, lightweight, durable, non-branded items with clear utility and low regulatory complexity.
4. Compare at least two suppliers for the same specification.
5. Ask for current product photos/video, dimensions, materials, packaging, country of dispatch, tracking, defect policy, return address and written fulfilment terms.
6. Calculate landed cost per destination country before publishing.
7. Publish only after supplier identity and product claims are verified.

## Supplier outreach message

Subject: Dropshipping fulfilment enquiry — GOD LEGACY

Hello,

We are preparing GOD LEGACY, an online retail brand focused on practical everyday, travel-organisation and fitness accessories. We are evaluating fulfilment partners and would like to understand your dropshipping terms.

For the products we shortlist, please confirm:
1. Do you support one-piece dropshipping with no minimum order?
2. Are there any registration, monthly, listing, handling or per-order platform fees?
3. Can you ship blind/direct to customers in the US, UK, EU, India and other supported countries? Please list excluded destinations.
4. For each SKU, what are the product cost, shipping cost, dispatch time and realistic delivery estimate?
5. Which warehouse/country will each item ship from?
6. Is end-to-end tracking included?
7. What happens if an item is defective, lost, delayed or returned? Who pays return shipping?
8. Can you provide current product photos/specifications and confirm that you are authorised to supply the product?
9. When is payment due, and what payment methods are accepted?
10. Can you provide a sample quote without requiring a paid subscription or deposit?

We will only list products after verifying the product, total landed cost and fulfilment terms.

Thank you,
GOD LEGACY

## Approval scorecard (complete for every exact SKU)

| Check | Pass requirement |
|---|---|
| Supplier identity | Business name and contact independently checked |
| Product rights | No counterfeit, copied brand or unauthorised logo |
| Specification | Exact materials, dimensions, variants and photos |
| Stock | Current availability confirmed |
| Landed cost | Product + shipping + duties/tax assumptions documented |
| Delivery | Dispatch and delivery estimates confirmed for each destination |
| Tracking | Valid tracking method confirmed |
| Defects/returns | Written policy and return address obtained |
| Cash flow | Merchant can pay supplier before payout; otherwise do not sell |
| Margin | Positive margin after gateway, shipping, tax, refunds and currency costs |
